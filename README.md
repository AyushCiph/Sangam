# 🌍 SANGAM — Where Challenges Meet Solvers

> **Smart India Hackathon 2026 | Team Project**

## 💡 About

**SANGAM** is a citizen-driven platform designed to help people report real-world societal challenges and connect them with universities, industries, organisations, and relevant authorities to develop practical solutions.

**See it. Share it. Improve it.**

## 📌 Problem Statement

**SIH Problem Statement ID:** SIH26043

> A digital platform to crowdsource societal challenges and facilitate collaborative problem solving through universities and industry partnerships.

**Theme:** Disaster Management
**Category:** Software

## ✨ Key Features

* 📢 Citizen-driven challenge reporting
* 🔍 Evidence-based problem sharing
* 🎓 University and student collaboration
* 🏢 Industry and organisation partnerships
* 📋 RTI and solution guidance
* 🔄 Transparent issue tracking
* 💡 Research and innovation opportunities

## 🛠️ Technologies

* Progressive Web App (PWA)
